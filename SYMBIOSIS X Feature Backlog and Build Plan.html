Definition of done and release checks

Before merging a ticket

Name the dependency and affected consumer. Attach the passing fixture or test. Have the owning team review the diff. Verify model logic at the API boundary or UI behavior in the actual screen, depending on the ticket. Record known gaps with their own issue.

Before tagging the release

Verify nominal, stale advice and fresh retry from a clean checkout. Test exact Ground snapshot values and private-field exclusion. Rehearse both browser sessions on one host and the offline backup. Test playback and English subtitles before final submission.

Golden replay. Five display beats at 00:00, 01:00, 08:00, 10:00 and 13:00. Relay also delivers at 06:00 and 15:00. At 13:00 Crew is on v103 with 0.20 kW and Ground remains on v102 with 0.60 kW. The stale advisory produces zero applied effects. The separate fresh approval test applies once.
